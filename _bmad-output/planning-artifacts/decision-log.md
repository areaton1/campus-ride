### Note on graded BMAD Decision Log vs architecture AD-*

MIS 430’s graded **BMAD Decision Log** records where the team changed, overrode, or rejected BMAD skill output (compiled from weekly status reports). The **AD-AI-*** and **AD-PLACE-*** rows above are **technical architecture decisions** for implementation consistency (same pattern as AD-ISO / AD-POI). They are **not** fabricated graded override entries. Jade owns whether any item also belongs in the course Decision Log deliverable.
